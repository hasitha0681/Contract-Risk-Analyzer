# AtliQ — Contract Review Principles

*(Written down from the management team for the Contract Risk Analyzer, October 2026. AtliQ has no fixed checklist; each contract is judged fresh, aiming for a win-win deal. These principles are what "judged fresh" means in practice. They apply whether AtliQ is the supplier, the buyer, a partner or either party to an NDA.)*

## P1. Win-win terms
Terms should be balanced for both sides. Flag one-sided clauses, including in AtliQ's own templates when AtliQ is the buyer (agencies and freelancers should not receive terms AtliQ would reject from a client). Typical one-sided clauses: indemnity for the other side's own negligence, obligations or caps that bind only one party, open-ended liquidated damages, payment terms beyond 45 days, pay-when-paid.

## P2. Mutual NDAs
AtliQ prefers not to sign a one-sided NDA. An NDA titled "mutual" whose obligations bind only one side is one-sided. An NDA should not carry a non-compete or a long non-solicit.

## P3. Risk in proportion to value
Judge liability and penalties against the contract value. Example: a 12-month contract worth $2,000 a month ($24,000) with $500,000 liability is a deal in bad faith. Unlimited liability is never acceptable. Liquidated damages should be capped.

## P4. Never break a past promise
A new contract must not conflict with restrictions AtliQ accepted in signed contracts: non-competes, exclusivity, pricing promises (most-favoured-customer rates) and IP already given away.

## P5. Healthcare: BAA first
For any healthcare project, a Business Associate Agreement must be signed first, with the client and with every freelancer or subcontractor who works on it. No patient data is touched before that.

## P6. Right AtliQ entity
US clients contract with AtliQ Inc. All other countries contract with AtliQ Technologies Pvt Ltd.

## P7. Pay for work done
If the contract ends early, AtliQ should be paid for work already done, including work in progress.

## P8. Client-caused delays
Delay penalties should not apply to delays the client causes.
